What's actually broken

It's the gearbox — not the door.

Modern UPVC doors lock by lifting the handle, which engages a long metal strip running the full length of the door edge — the multipoint mechanism. Inside it sits a small gearbox that translates the handle motion into bolt-throwing motion. It's the gearbox that fails first, almost without exception.

Symptoms: the handle gets stiffer over months, then suddenly won't lift to lock; or it lifts but the door won't latch shut; or the key turns but doesn't throw the bolts. None of these mean you need a new door. They mean you need a new gearbox, which costs a fraction of a door, and we can fit one in 30–60 minutes on a single visit.

Brands and models we carry

The common ones on the van: GU Ferco, Yale, Avocet, Maco, Fuhr, Mila, Roto, Winkhaus, ERA, Avantis, plus the standard 35mm and 45mm backset cylinder cases. If we don't have your exact part on the van, we'll source it the same day from a local supplier and book the fit for that afternoon or first thing the next morning.

While we're there

We upgrade the cylinder to a 3-star Kitemark anti-snap as standard (the snap-resistant kind that breaks at a sacrificial point rather than letting a burglar pull the cylinder out). It's the cheapest meaningful security upgrade you can make to a UPVC door. We'll quote the upgrade with the gearbox repair.

Common questions

Most-asked, answered straight.

How much does UPVC lock repair cost?
Depends on the brand of mechanism and whether you want the anti-snap cylinder upgrade at the same time. Ring with the make of mechanism if you know it (sometimes printed on the strip when you open the door) — we'll quote you a fixed price on the phone.
Can you fix it same-visit?
In 90% of cases yes. We carry the common gearboxes (GU Ferco, Yale, Avocet, Maco, Fuhr, Mila, Roto, Winkhaus, ERA, Avantis) on the van. For an unusual part we source same-day from a local supplier and book the fit for that afternoon.
Do I need a new door?
Almost certainly not. The door's fine; the mechanism inside it is the part that fails. Replacing the mechanism costs a fraction of replacing the door.
Is anti-snap really worth it?
Yes. Lock-snapping is the most common UPVC break-in method — burglars grab the protruding cylinder with mole grips and snap it. A 3-star Kitemark anti-snap cylinder has a sacrificial section that breaks first, leaving the rest of the lock intact and secure. We fit them as standard.
Will it look the same after?
Yes. The repair is internal to the door — the handle, faceplate and visible hardware are reused (unless they're damaged). New parts only on the inside.
Do you do this in Huddersfield and Barnsley too?
Yes. One-hour target across HD and S70–S75 postcodes. Same fixed-price-on-the-phone rule.
