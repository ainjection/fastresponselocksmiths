Our Services

Auto Locksmith Services

Fully mobile vehicle locksmith covering Wakefield and the whole WF postcode area. We come to you with the latest tools and equipment.

Car Lockout Assistance

Locked your keys in the car? Our local rapid response team will gain entry to your car absolutely damage free using specialist LISHI tools and lock pick sets.

Lost Car Key Replacement

Lost all your car keys? We cut and programme brand new keys at your location. No expensive dealer visit, no towing — just a fast, affordable solution on the spot.

Chip Keys & Remote Fobs

We programme transponder chip keys and remote key fobs for a wide range of vehicles. Our fully equipped mobile unit means we come to you — no towing required.

Ignition Repair & Replacement

Key stuck or won't turn? Broken key snapped in the ignition? We diagnose and repair ignition barrel problems on-site, quickly and affordably.

Key Cutting

Need a spare cut? We provide precision key cutting for all vehicle types at your location. Get a spare made now before you're caught out in an emergency.

Vans & Commercial Vehicles

We cover vans, trucks, and commercial vehicles of all sizes. Fleet lockout or a replacement key for your work van — we've got it covered.
