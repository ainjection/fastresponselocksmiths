What we do, in order

Secure first. Paperwork second.

If you've been broken into the priority is making the property secure again — so you can sleep tonight, so the burglars can't come back, and so your insurance claim isn't compromised by a second loss. We get to you the same day, often within the hour during normal times.

Step 1 — Make it safe

Whatever entry point was used (forced UPVC, snapped cylinder, kicked panel, smashed window) we secure first. New locks where the existing ones were defeated. Boarding or interim glazing for broken glass. Temporary security on damaged frames if a permanent repair will take a day longer to source parts.

Step 2 — Replace to insurance-approved standards

Locks are replaced to BS3621 grade (the British Standard insurers require for "approved" status). 3-star Kitemark anti-snap cylinders fitted as standard — these are the cylinders that defeat the most common UPVC attack method (lock-snapping). Where door or frame structure was damaged we repair it; where it's beyond repair we'll quote the replacement.

Step 3 — Document for your insurer

We issue a written invoice itemising the work, and a certificate confirming the new locks are to BS3621 / insurance-approved standard. Your insurer accepts these as proof of remediation — they're standard documents in their claim process. No hassle, no follow-up calls from them to us.

Step 4 — Suggest upgrades, don't push them

We'll tell you honestly where else the property is exposed (back door cylinders, window catches, garage side door, etc.) and quote the upgrade cost. No upsell. If you'd rather not do it now we'll fit it next time you ring.

Common questions

Most-asked, answered straight.

Do I need to call my insurer first?
No — call us first if the property is open or not secure. Make the property safe is always step 1. You can call the insurer once you're inside and able to sit down. We provide the paperwork they need afterwards.
What is "insurance-approved standard"?
Locks meeting BS3621 (the British Standard for thief-resistant locks) plus appropriate supplementary measures (anti-snap cylinders on UPVC, deadbolts on wooden doors, etc.). We fit to that grade by default and provide a written certificate confirming it.
How fast can you get there?
Same day in normal circumstances. For an active emergency (you're standing outside an open property) we'll prioritise and arrive as fast as the route allows — typically inside the hour for the WF postcode area, one hour for HD and S70–S75.
Will my insurer pay for this?
Most insurance policies cover post-burglary security repair under "emergency cover" or as a claimable expense within the claim. Keep the invoice and certificate we give you — they're what the insurer needs to reimburse the cost.
What if my door is too damaged to repair?
We'll secure the property tonight with interim measures (boarding, temporary locks, supplementary security) and quote a permanent door or frame replacement for the next working day. The interim work is included so you're not exposed overnight.
Can I have anti-snap fitted now to prevent it?
Yes — that's a Home Security Check. We come out, assess your existing cylinders and recommend upgrades. 3-star Kitemark anti-snap is the single biggest defence against the most common UPVC break-in method. Cheap to fit when there's no emergency.
