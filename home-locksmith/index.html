Common questions

Most-asked, answered straight.

My UPVC door won't lock — can you fix it without replacing the door?
Almost always yes. The mechanism inside the door (the multipoint gearbox) is the part that fails — we replace it without touching the door itself. Most repairs are done same-visit because we carry the common parts on the van.
How much to change all the locks after moving house?
For an average three-bedroom semi expect £180–£320 fully fitted, depending on whether you want everything keyed alike, the cylinder grade (anti-snap is standard from us) and the door types. We quote you the fixed price on the phone before we set off.
Do you provide insurance certificates for post-burglary work?
Yes. We issue a written receipt and a certificate confirming the work was done to insurance-approved standards (BS3621 compliant cylinders, supplementary locks where appropriate). Your insurer accepts these as proof of remediation.
Are anti-snap cylinders worth it?
Yes, and they're our standard. Lock-snapping is the most common method for UPVC break-ins. A 1-star or 3-star Kitemark anti-snap cylinder breaks deliberately at a sacrificial point that leaves the lock secure rather than open. Cost is £30–£60 per cylinder fitted — the cheapest meaningful security upgrade you can make.
Can you fit smart locks?
Yes. We supply and fit Yale, Avia, Era and Ultion digital locks (and equivalents). Includes setup, code generation, user training and integration with any compatible bridge if you want remote access. Brand-agnostic — we'll tell you honestly which suits your door and use-case.
Will I be in for a long wait at 2am?
No. Out-of-hours is the same service as daytime — we answer the phone, give you an ETA, set off. Same price. No call-out loading.
