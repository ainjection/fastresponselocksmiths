Common questions

Most-asked, answered straight.

How much does it cost to replace a lost car key in Wakefield?
Most modern cars are £140–£260 fully cut and programmed at your location — significantly less than a main dealer. We give you the exact price for your make, model and year over the phone before we set off. Specialist or luxury vehicles (BMW, Audi, Range Rover etc.) sit at the upper end. We carry the equipment and blanks for the vast majority of vehicles on UK roads.
Can you open my car without damaging it?
Yes. We use LISHI tools and specialist lock-pick sets — the same non-destructive equipment manufacturers and main dealers use. There is no damage to the lock, the door frame or the paint. If we can't open it without damage we don't charge.
How long will you take to get to me?
Our target is 30 minutes inside the Wakefield postcode area (WF1–WF17) and up to one hour for the surrounding West Yorkshire towns. Traffic, time of day and the queue ahead of you can shift this — we'll give you a realistic ETA on the phone.
Do you work on vans, BMWs, Audis, Fords, all makes?
Yes. We carry the equipment and blanks for the vast majority of cars, vans and light commercial vehicles in the UK. BMW, Audi, Mercedes, VW, Ford, Vauxhall, Peugeot, Renault, Citroen, Toyota, Honda, Nissan, Land Rover, Ford Transit, Vauxhall Vivaro, Mercedes Sprinter — and many more. Ring with the make, model and year and we'll confirm before we set off.
Is there a call-out charge?
No. We quote you a fixed price for the whole job before we leave the workshop. That's the price you pay, regardless of how long it takes us. No surprise extras and no incremental hourly charge.
Do you take card payments?
Yes — all major debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express). Cash is fine too. Payment after the job is done, never before.
Are you available at night and on bank holidays?
Yes. 24 hours a day, seven days a week, 365 days a year. The same locksmith answers the phone at 3am as at 3pm. Out-of-hours rates are not loaded — same price, same service.
